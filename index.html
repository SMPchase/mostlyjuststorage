Before you ask

A few straight answers.

Where exactly is the studio?

In Havre de Grace, Maryland. The exact address is shared with confirmed bookings and event guests when appropriate.

What gear is included?

The setup can change by session. Tell us what you need and we’ll confirm the available room, gear, inputs, and monitoring before you commit.

Does this form confirm my booking?

No. It sends a real inquiry to the studio. You’ll get an email reply before the date, scope, or price is considered confirmed.

How do I hear about new shows?

Published dates appear above. Short-notice announcements also go to Instagram.
